WISS Stundenplan 1.12.0
What's new since 1.10.1

NEW LOOK
- New logo, with its own version for every style (also the toolbar icon in the browser)
- A proper look for the Standard style: new fonts, warmer colours, softer cards.
- Apple style with the new glass look and a floating tab bar.
- Style previews in the settings follow light or dark mode.
- Much less text everywhere.

OVERVIEW AT THE TOP OF EVERY TAB
- Week: the lesson now or next with the room in big numbers, today as a strip, Monday to Friday at a glance.
- The next exam in the week card can be hidden.
- Exams: countdown to the next exam, grouped by month.
- Grades: average, modules done, finished modules as tiles.
- Absences: what to excuse first, then how many lessons you can still miss.
- Rooms: the rooms that stay free the longest at the top.

SYNC BETWEEN YOUR DEVICES (OPTIONAL)
- Notes, exam corrections and a few settings, end-to-end encrypted. Off until you turn it on.
- Link a device with a short code that works once for 10 minutes.

PRIVACY
- A short privacy page on the first start, also under Einstellungen, Über.
